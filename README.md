# EoA-Repo

Public media for Eve of Alana social posts.

Rules (from Juan, 14 Oct 2026):
- Allowed: photos and images that are already published or harmless to publish, short social clips (a few seconds, made for posts), and documents that reveal nothing about the band.
- Never full songs, audio releases, full-length videos, or anything the band sells.
- Nothing internal: no plans, drafts, contacts, numbers, or personal data.
